OLDSelling landing page

Files:
- index.html: page content and structure
- styles.css: responsive visual styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, demo poll and email draft behavior

Before publishing:
1. Open script.js and replace hello@oldselling.in with the email address you want visitors to contact.
2. The feedback form does not save submissions to a database. It opens the visitor's default email app with a prefilled message.
3. The Good idea / Needs work poll is a browser-only demo. It does not collect central vote counts. To collect votes across visitors, connect a form or survey service.
4. The page loads Google Fonts from Google Fonts. A system font fallback is included.

To preview:
Open index.html in a browser.

To deploy:
Upload index.html, styles.css and script.js to your Netlify site (keep all three in the same folder).
